# poeticWare
generative poetry homebrew for 3DS

(using this readme as my own personal diary)

inspired by generative/computation poetics work that i've been exposed to on the internet. Stumbling upon the If/Then technologicy & poetics online group thing was a big deal for me!
A workshop there was where I first learned about RiTa & small language models, the core ideas of which I've implemented myself here.

I enjoy the shared experience of created a poem with poeticWare, it's a balance I've been looking for in a lot of my work.
How can you allow audiences to really feel in control of the output while it still feeling like YOU, your work...
Idk I think I hit that with this one. Would love to watch more people play with it, I'm unsure how to get it online and open it up
to different modes of interaction away from the 3DS, but that's what I'm looking at next. It'll be pretty simple to get this
working on a webpage or itch.io, I think...

Another really enjoyable part of this project for me is when the grammar is stupid and doesn't make sense.
it adds a layer to the deciphering of words and the lines in relation to each other that makes the poems very unique and
also I've found myself making big leaps to make sense of the words in front of me.
feels like everyone that reads this would also make those big leaps, but in doing so
would make each poem very specific to their personality.

interesting....
